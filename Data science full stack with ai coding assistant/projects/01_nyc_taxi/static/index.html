How long will this ride take?

A dispatcher quotes a time at booking. Too optimistic and riders churn; too pessimistic and they book elsewhere. So the product needs an estimate and an honest range, using only what is known before the car arrives.

Success criterion fixed before modeling: beat the "distance ÷ typical speed" rule of thumb on MAE, and make the 90% range actually contain the true time ~90% of the time on a week the model never saw.

Correlation with duration

Fare, total and tip correlate strongly with duration because the meter charges by time. None of them exist at booking time, so they are excluded. See the leakage demo in Evaluation.

Cleaning rules (decided before modeling)

Temporal split: the future is never used to predict the past

Hyperparameters are chosen on the tune week, interval widths on the calibration days, and the final week is scored exactly once.

Features

allowed: known at booking

pickup/dropoff zone & borough, hour (+ sin/cos), day of week, weekend, same zone, passengers, taxi colour, route distance (from the trip planner; the app fills in the historical median for the route).

excluded: leaks the answer

fare, tip, tolls, total are all computed from the finished trip. dropoff time is the label itself.
