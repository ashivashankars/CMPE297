How much power will Germany use over the next two weeks?

Grid operators buy and schedule generation days ahead. Under-forecast and they pay spot-market premiums; over-forecast and they pay for idle reserve. They need a point forecast and a margin whose stated confidence is true.

Every number on this page comes from a rolling-origin backtest: 51 weekly forecast origins across 2017, each forecasting 14 days ahead using only data available at that origin. 2017 was never used for training, tuning or calibration.

Features for a forecast made at origin t for day t+h

known at tlag_0…lag_6 (last 7 days up to t), 7/28-day means, 7-day volatility, mean of the last 4 same-weekdays ≤ t, same weekday a year before target (only if ≤ t)
known in advancetarget's weekday, month, season (sin/cos day-of-year), public holiday, bridge day, Christmas week
designhorizon h = 1…14 as a feature: one global "direct" model, no recursive error compounding
neverany value after t: e.g. "yesterday" relative to a target 10 days out
