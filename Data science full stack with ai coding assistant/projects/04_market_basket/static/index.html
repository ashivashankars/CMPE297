What goes in the basket together, and what should we suggest next?

A grocery chain wants (a) product pairings for shelf placement and promotions, and (b) a "you might also need…" prompt at checkout. Association rules answer (a) directly. For (b), the rules have to beat the obvious strategy of always suggesting the best-sellers.

Headline finding: high-confidence rules mostly predict whole milk and vegetables, which "most popular" already suggests. They tie the popularity baseline. A tuned item co-occurrence model wins, especially on long-tail items. Full numbers in Evaluation.
