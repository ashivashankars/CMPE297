What does it take to build a language model, end to end, on a laptop?

The goal is understanding, not scale: build every piece by hand (tokenizer, transformer, training loop, sampler), train it on Shakespeare on 2 CPU cores, and measure it honestly against simple baselines. Then test whether 2023-era building blocks (RoPE, RMSNorm, SwiGLU) beat the 2019 GPT-2 recipe at equal size.

Success criteria: validation loss far below an n-gram model on text the model never trained on; a stable, non-diverging training curve; samples that pick up Shakespeare's form (speaker names, verse lines, archaic words).
